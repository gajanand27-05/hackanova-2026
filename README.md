# HACKANOVA 2026

Official website for **HACKANOVA 2026**, a 12-hour inter-college hackathon organised by the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, KNS Institute of Technology, Bengaluru.

- **When:** 26 November 2026, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST (code freeze at 6:00 PM)
- **Where:** KNS Institute of Technology, Bengaluru
- **Registration:** via Unstop (link coming soon)

> Work in progress. The site is being built with React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Three.js and GSAP, and will be deployed on Vercel.
